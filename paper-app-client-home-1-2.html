Rules for devs (T-7), paper-app clients only

  • Info banner at the top with the wording shown. It matches the client creation email.
  • Step 2 shows a green check and "Completed on paper". No "Start Application" button, no enrollment validation. (Clients see "Completed on paper"; admin/agent screens show "N/A – Paper App", per T-6.)
  • Progress circle counts Step 2 as done for paper clients, so it doesn't look stuck.
  • Step 3 stays locked until the proposal reaches the client (after agent approval), with the line "Available once the carrier returns your proposal. We'll email you."
  • "Estimate My Benefits" is hidden from the menu for paper clients (no estimator illustrations).
  • Step 1 stays open, with two clearly labelled buttons side by side (not stacked): "Review my details" (opens their profile/enrollment details) and "Upload documents" (opens Document Upload). This replaces the vague "Edit" + "Upload More Documents" link. The client is never locked out.
  • No "Confirm my details" button. The admin's required fields + Application Kit decide when the case moves on.
  • Step 4 is unchanged.
