Rules for devs (T-5)

  • Show the tag when the client's policy is a paper app (carrier Securian/Symetra, product Kai-Zen Paper Workflow, created via Add Paper Application).
  • Tag text: PAPER APP. Display only, not clickable.
  • Places: (1) agent Client Management list, next to the name; (2) agent's client page header.
  • The tag stays permanently. It records how the case came in, even after the client signs.
  • No new "Paper Apps" filter for agents in this task.
  • Admin-side tags are covered in other tasks (action items, client page).
