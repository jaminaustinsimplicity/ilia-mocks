Dev notes · the flow

  1. Creating a paper app (T-1) adds the action item "Complete Paper App Details" with a PAPER tag.
  2. "Complete Details" takes the admin to that client's page (no pop-up).
  3. The client page shows a Paper App box explaining what to do, plus ACTION REQUIRED on the Enrollment Details and Documents tabs.
  4. Enrollment Details: admin fills the required minimum fields. Judy will define which fields.
  5. Documents → Upload: the dropdown gains an "Application Kit" group. Doc names are pending the Securian / Symetra kits Judy is requesting.
  6. When both tabs are complete, the action item completes on its own, and "Mark Application Sent to Carrier" (T-8) is created.
