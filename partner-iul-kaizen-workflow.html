Partner IUL + Kai-Zen Boost: Workflow

Outside groups (not part of Simplicity) market IULs to their prospects. Kai-Zen is shown as the "boost" for anyone who qualifies. All marketing and tracking happens outside ILIA. ILIA is only the enrollment engine, and every client lands on an existing ILIA agent.

Admin layer Define later

What crosses the wall Open

Likely contents of the handoff into ILIA, to confirm:

  • Product choice: IUL or Kai-Zen, plus the confirmed numbers
  • Prospect basics: name, email, phone, state
  • Calculator answers, so the prospect doesn't re-enter them
  • Agent of record (from the member's selection)
  • Group / member / link reference, if ILIA needs to keep it

Status back to the CMS? Open

Does ILIA report progress after the wall?

  • A. Nothing comes back. Tracking stops at the decision / account creation
  • B. Stage only: account created → enrolling → issued (no personal or policy data)
  • C. Full enrollment and policy detail

Attribution chain

How every client is traced from the marketing to the agent of record.

Group→ Leader→ Member→ Link / asset ID→ Prospect funnel→ IUL or Kai-Zen→ ILIA account→ Member's selected agent
The member picks their agent in the CMS, so the agent of record is decided per member, not per group.
