The Phase Your Policy Is Entering

Your Kai-Zen policy is entering its eleventh year — an important phase of the original design. The focus now shifts toward the policy's cash-value growth, potential supplemental retirement income, and long-term death benefit.

The original illustration included a planned term-rider death benefit reduction. The term rider was used to lower the early cost of insurance and let more of your premium build cash value. Because the term rider costs more in later years, reducing it now is intended to keep insurance costs lower and support future growth and income. The choice is yours — and we will not make any change without your signed election below.

Important considerations

Once the term rider is reduced, it generally cannot be restored to its previous level. Restoring coverage later may require new underwriting and could be more expensive or unavailable on the same terms.

Because this is a premium-financed policy, keeping a higher level of term coverage may raise future costs, which could reduce policy values and increase the risk of a policy lapse in later years. The decision is a trade-off between death-benefit protection, cash-value growth, supplemental income, and long-term cost.
