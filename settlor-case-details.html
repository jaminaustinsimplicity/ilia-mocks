Settlor & Authorized Signer

Beneficiaries▸

What changed from today's screen

  1. "Master Settlor" is gone. Master Settlor badge, + Add Master Settlor, Associated Policies Under Master Settlor.
  2. Every policy answers three questions: is the insured the settlor, if not is it an individual or an entity, and what type of entity.
  3. The authorized signer is always stated, even when it's simply the client.
  4. One settlor record can be linked to many policies ("Policies linked to this settlor"), so an entity is entered once.
  5. Portal access sits on each signer (an on/off switch plus invite status), not on a "master settlor" account.

How portal access works

  1. Signers: admin turns portal access on for whichever signers should log in, then sends invites (one at a time or all at once). Access covers every linked policy. Status shows not invited, invite sent, active (last login) or turned off.
  2. Insureds who are not the settlor: no access by default. Admin can turn it on for a single policy, which emails that insured an invite. They see that policy only.
  3. Turning access off is silent and can be undone. Turning it back on sends the access-restored email.
  4. Already partly built: the unmerged settlor code has a per-policy switch for insured access, off by default and emailing the insured when turned on, plus invite, first-login and restricted dates per user. Dev to confirm.
