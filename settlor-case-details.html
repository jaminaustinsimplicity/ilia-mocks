What changed from today's screen

  1. "Master Settlor" is gone. Master Settlor badge, + Add Master Settlor, Associated Policies Under Master Settlor.
  2. Every policy answers three questions: is the insured the settlor, if not is it an individual or an entity, and what type of entity.
  3. The authorized signer is always stated, even when it's simply the client.
  4. One settlor record can be linked to many policies ("Policies linked to this settlor"), so an entity is entered once.
  5. Portal access sits on the one authorized signer (an on/off switch plus invite status), not on a "master settlor" account.

How portal access works

  1. One authorized signer per settlor (confirmed 10/26, including entities). All forms and documents go to that one person.
  2. Insured is the settlor: no invite. ILIA opens the standard client Servicing Portal automatically once enrollment is closed, the policy is in force and a tranche is assigned (existing ILIA rule).
  3. Separate settlor: admin clicks Invite Authorized Signer to Servicing Portal, confirms the person, policies and email, and sends. The signer creates an ILIA account and password, then lands in the Servicing Settlor (Authorized Signer) Portal. Policies not yet in force appear once in force. Resend invite is available until the account is created. Access covers every linked policy. Invite progress shows each step with its date: invite sent → invite opened → account created → logged in.
  4. Insureds who are not the settlor: no access by default. Admin can turn it on for a single policy, which emails that insured an invite. They see that policy only.
  5. Turning access off emails the person (#62) and can be undone. Turning it back on sends the access-restored email (#43 signer, #63 insured). HR users get no access-off email.
  6. Already partly built: the unmerged settlor code has a per-policy switch for insured access, off by default and emailing the insured when turned on, plus invite-sent, first-login and restricted dates per user. "Invite opened" is likely new (needs email-open tracking from the email service). Dev to confirm.

Linking a policy (rules)

  1. A policy has exactly one settlor. Linking it here makes this settlor its settlor and this signer its authorized signer. It is the same change as using Edit settlor on that policy.
  2. Can link: in-force policies whose settlor today is the insured. The review step shows exactly what changes.
  3. Can't link (shown with the reason):
    • Already linked to this settlor.
    • Linked to a different settlor. Unlink it there first; ILIA never moves a policy silently.
    • Surrendered or deceased.
    • Has documents out for signature.
  4. Insured access: if the insured used the portal as the settlor, admin chooses to keep view access or turn it off. Otherwise it is off by default.
  5. Unlink: the policy goes back to "the insured is the settlor". To pick someone else, use Edit settlor on that policy.
  6. Every link and unlink is logged in the client's activity history.
