IUL Selling Funnel: Design Draft

This is what a prospect sees after a group member shares a link or QR code. A few short questions lead to their own IUL numbers, which they can adjust. The same numbers are shown with the Kai-Zen boost. They choose a plan (IUL or Kai-Zen plus their numbers) and land on ILIA account creation with that plan carried over. At any point they can learn more and come back to their numbers. Designed phone-first, since most people will arrive by QR, with a wider web layout.

The flow

The main path is 7 steps. "Learn more" and "Talk to my agent" are side paths that always return to the prospect's numbers. A stepper at the top of the funnel lets the prospect jump back to their answers, numbers or plan at any time before the account is created.

Entry
Link or QR

From a group member. It carries the member and asset ID, so every step is tracked.

Step 1
Short questions

5 quick screens of taps, plus where to send results. About 60 seconds.

Step 2
Your numbers

Calculator results and a growth chart. Adjust the yearly amount, years and start age, and results update live.

Step 3
Kai-Zen boost

The same numbers with leverage, side by side, for prospects who qualify.

Step 4
Choose plan

Pick IUL or Kai-Zen straight from the results, then review and confirm.

Step 5 · The wall
Handoff

Product, numbers, prospect and agent of record pass to ILIA.

Step 6 · ILIA
Create account

Pre-filled with what they've already told us. Their chosen plan is shown alongside.

Step 7 · ILIA
Enrollment starts

Next steps for the plan they chose, guided by their agent.

Learn more opens the deep content from the current IUL marketing: how an IUL works, taxes, safety, borrowing, the Kai-Zen boost (with their own eligibility) and FAQs. Every section links back to "see it in my numbers", and "Back to my numbers" / "Choose my plan" stay pinned at the bottom.
Talk to my agent (always in the header) shows the agent card and a call request. The agent is the member's selected ILIA agent, and the request is tracked to the member.

The short question set

This is the fewest questions that can still (a) produce real calculator numbers, (b) check Kai-Zen eligibility and (c) learn what the prospect cares about. Everything else gets a sensible default the prospect can adjust on the results screen.
