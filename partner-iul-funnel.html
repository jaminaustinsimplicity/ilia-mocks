IUL Selling Funnel: Design Draft

This is what a prospect sees after a group member shares a link or QR code. A few short questions lead to their own IUL numbers, which they can adjust. The same numbers are shown with the Kai-Zen boost. Then they either confirm (choose IUL or Kai-Zen plus their numbers) and go to ILIA account creation, or they learn more. Designed phone-first, since most people will arrive by QR, with a wider web layout.

The flow

The main path is 6 steps. "Learn more" and "Talk to my agent" are side paths, and both return to the prospect's numbers.

Entry
Link or QR

From a group member. It carries the member and asset ID, so every step is tracked.

Step 1
Short questions

5 quick screens of taps, plus where to send results. About 60 seconds.

Step 2
Your IUL numbers

Calculator results from their answers.

Step 3
Play with the numbers

Change the yearly amount, years paying and income start age. Results update live.

Step 4
Kai-Zen boost

The same numbers with leverage, side by side, for prospects who qualify.

Step 5
Confirm

Choose IUL or Kai-Zen and lock the numbers.

Step 6 · The wall
ILIA account creation

Product, numbers and agent of record go to ILIA for enrollment.

Learn more (from results) opens the deep content: how an IUL works, taxes, safety, borrowing, how the Kai-Zen boost works, and FAQs. A "Back to my numbers" button stays visible.
Talk to my agent (always in the header) shows the agent card and a call request. The agent is the member's selected ILIA agent, and the request is tracked to the member.

The short question set

This is the fewest questions that can still (a) produce real calculator numbers, (b) check Kai-Zen eligibility and (c) learn what the prospect cares about. Everything else gets a sensible default the prospect can adjust on the results screen.
