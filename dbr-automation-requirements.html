DBR Automation Requirements

Automating the Death Benefit Reduction (DBR) process for Kai-Zen IUL policies in Policy Years 11–15. Today it's run manually by ops; this project makes ILIA proactively detect eligible policies, notify clients on a schedule, collect their election — the client picks one of 3 ready illustrations (Full / Half / No reduction) or a custom amount — via DocuSign, and let Marissa manage every case from Tranche Management. This page is the living requirements — built event by event.

What we're building — one card per event, left to right. We fully define & confirm each card before adding the next.

120, 90 & 60 days before the deadline

2 Client → ILIA · DocuSign Draft · confirm

Client Responds & Elects

The client opens the DocuSign packet, picks one option, and signs. ILIA reads the choice and routes it automatically.

How ILIA reads the response
  • →DocuSign completion webhook fires when the client signs
  • →ILIA reads dbr_election = full / half / none (+ requested_amount if custom)
  • →Stops the 90 / 60-day reminders
  • →Updates the DBR status in Tranche Management
The routes — 3 picks + custom (+ no response)
FULL
Full reduction — apply the full amount→ Accepted → Applied (Event 3)
HALF
Half reduction — apply the ~50% amount→ Accepted → Applied (Event 3)
NONE
No reduction — no change this year→ Declined — re-offer (through Yr 15)
CUST
Custom amount — a different number→ Flagged for NIW · handled offline (MVP)
—
No response by the deadline→ No response · re-offer next year

Full / Half / No reduction are pre-computed — one envelope, one signature. Custom is rare, so it's handled offline (no ILIA automation).

4 ILIA · DocuSign · Carrier Draft · confirm

Fulfillment — Trustee & Carrier

After a Full or Half election, ILIA drives the signed reduction through the trustee and on to the carrier — every step visible in Tranche Management. (New scope, Judy Aug 19.)

The document journey
  • →Carrier forms filled with the elected reduction amount — they ride in the same envelope but only surface to the carrier at their step
  • →Envelope routes to the Trustee / Trust first
  • →The trust signs the change form (sign-off)
  • →Packet is released to the Carrier
  • →Confirm the carrier received the final forms (via feeds, if available)

The amount the client picked must pull into the carrier forms — either auto-populated from the selection or entered by admin (open question below).
