Dev notes · T-8

  1. The "Mark Application Sent to Carrier" action item appears once "Complete Paper App Details" auto-completes (T-3/T-4).
  2. The admin sends the application to the carrier outside ILIA. ILIA sends nothing to the carrier.
  3. "Mark Sent" opens a pop-up: Date sent (required, defaults to today), Note (optional), Confirmation attachment (optional).
  4. Confirm completes the item, sets "Application Submitted to Carrier" with that date, and saves the note and attachment to the client's record.
  5. The next item, "Upload & Send Proposal" (T-9), is created right away and waits for the carrier's proposal.
  6. The client's Enrollment Home banner changes to the "submitted on {date}" wording.
  7. No emails at this step.
